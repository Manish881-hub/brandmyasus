Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026 · Template — have a local lawyer review before accepting payments.

Short version: this demo collects nothing. When you go live, you’ll need an email + payment reference to run bids, publish only brand/logo/URL/amount, never publish emails, set no tracking cookies, and delete on request.

Who runs this

BrandMyAsus is run by you (sole trader). Add your name, address and contact email here. You are the data controller.

What you’ll collect when you launch

  • Email — bid confirmations, outbid alerts, winner contact. Required.
  • Brand name, logo file, website — published on the page + printed if approved.
  • Bid amount + time — published as part of the auction.
  • Payment reference from your provider (e.g. Stripe) — for refunds/invoices. Never store card numbers on this site.

What’s public

Approved bids publish brand, logo, URL and amount. Emails stay private. Pending bids stay private until approved.

Cookies / analytics

Recommended: no cookies, cookieless page-view counting only, plus localStorage for draft bid fields. Disclose your provider here (host, mail sender, payments, hosting logs).

Retention / rights

Keep bid records while the sticker is on the machine; delete logos + bids within 30 days on rejection or request. Keep invoices as long as tax law requires. EU users can request access, correction, deletion via your contact email within one month.
